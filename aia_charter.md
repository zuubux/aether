# Project Charter: Aether Interface Architecture (AIA)

## 1. High-Level Vision & Mission
We are designing a fundamentally new interaction paradigm between humans and technology (starting on the Linux desktop, but architecturally adaptable to tablets, spatial displays, and mobile)[cite: 3]. 

We reject legacy WIMP metaphors (Windows, Icons, Menus, Pointer) and rigid sci-fi HUD tropes[cite: 3]. Our goal is to build an interface tailored to human physiology, sensory intake, and subconscious intent—creating an interaction loop that feels like pure **intuition**[cite: 3].

---

## 2. Core Architectural Pillars
- **Anticipatory Intent:** Understand what the user wants before they execute manual labor[cite: 3].
- **Input-Agnostic Foundation:** The system must feel fluid, fast, and ergonomic with a standard keyboard and mouse first[cite: 3].
- **Ambient Intent Assist (`aia_saccade`):** Optional biometric signals (gaze tracking, head pose) act as passive intent lubricants—pre-warming target hitboxes and reducing cursor travel without triggering destructive actions (solving the "Midas Touch" problem)[cite: 3].
- **Underlying Semantic Graph (`aia_weaver`):** Files, notes, media, and tools are dynamically linked by semantic, explicit, and temporal relevance rather than trapped in rigid folder silos[cite: 3].
- **Visual Presentation Layer (`aia_canvas`):** A windowless, borderless spatial surface where content is projected as etched light on a calm obsidian void[cite: 3].

---

## 3. The Visual & Sensory Experience
- **Ambient Data Clouds:** Inactive tasks and background context drift softly in the periphery as gentle, flowing particles—never hidden in a taskbar, never demanding central attention[cite: 3].
- **Focal Condensation:** Where user focus settles (via cursor, intent pulse, or glance), data condenses into crisp, radiant focus[cite: 3].
- **Neuron-Like Tendrils:** Contextually related items gently materialize glowing, organic connections that draw supporting documents, notes, or media into the user's near focal orbit[cite: 3].
- **Aesthetic Tone:** Ethereal, organic, calm, and deeply comforting[cite: 3]. No harsh edges, no visual clutter, and zero window management overhead[cite: 3].

---

## 4. Engineering Stance
- **Never repeat legacy design just because "that's how it's always been done."**[cite: 3]
- **Keep it grounded:** Maintain compatibility with real Linux files (`.py`, `.txt`, `.md`, media) and underlying system performance[cite: 3].
