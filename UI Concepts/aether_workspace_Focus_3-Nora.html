FanX Salt Lake: Masks & Face Covering Guidelines

Full-face masks are permitted on the show floor at the Salt Palace, provided that the attendee maintains unobstructed peripheral vision and the mask can be easily removed at prop check-in or security checkpoints upon request. Eye mesh must not prevent navigation through convention crowds.

Smooth Porcelain Effect on EVA Foam

cosplay_kaylee Yesterday

For that eerie porcelain finish on the Puppet mask, don't use high-gloss spray—it reflects con hall lighting too harshly. Use a satin white lacquer, and rub the cheeks with watered-down magenta ink before the top seal dries.

Ideas for the Puppet Build
  • • Striped Sleeve Rig: Sew elastic loops inside the wrists so the extra-long puppet arms stay anchored during walking.
  • • Con-Floor Ventilation: Drill discreet pinholes right under the cheek curves to prevent mask fogging inside the Salt Palace.
